# BRAKMASRA storefront redesign

Reference sites: [Ochaka](https://ochaka-nuxtjs.vercel.app/?storefront=envato-elements) and [Anvogue](https://anvogue-html.vercel.app/?storefront=envato-elements).

The existing site used charcoal surfaces, a red accent, a forest hero, English/Sinhala translations, and a launch-gated merchandise catalog. The redesign preserves the brand, collection, routes, forms, SEO and launch behavior while replacing the visual storefront with fashion photography.

Design values: variance 6, motion 4, density 4. Native CSS with semantic light/dark tokens, existing Lucide icons, and Motion for scroll direction. Twelve-pixel photo corners, eight-pixel panels, pill calls to action and circular icon controls.

Homepage flow: fullscreen automatically rotating campaign, launch status below the opening viewport, filtered arrivals, collection tiles, brand editorial, four services, labelled sample review carousel, lookbook, newsletter, expanded footer. Header blends into the hero at the top; announcements scroll away, navigation hides going down and returns going up. Home is a direct link; Shop has three groups and two collection photographs in a full-width mega menu. Auto rotation continues during pointer hover and pauses for keyboard focus, hidden tabs, off-screen hero and reduced-motion preference, with an explicit pause/play control. White circular arrows appear at the two outer edges on hover.

Generated assets: `public/images/campaign.webp` and nine on-model photos in `public/images/models/`. Built-in image generation was used. Prompt specifications and original garment references are in `image-generation.json`; original product artwork stays in `public/images/products/`. Merchant-uploaded photography is not replaced.

Final campaign assets: `public/images/campaign-wide-4k.webp`, `campaign-black-4k.webp`, and `campaign-white-4k.webp`. Built-in generation supplied 1672×941 originals; the exports are upscaled to 3840 pixels wide with Lanczos resampling, WebP quality 96, and Next image delivery quality 95. They are not native 4K captures. The former portrait panels, contained-image seams, and small bottom-aligned mobile photographs were replaced with full-bleed photographs and responsive focal positions. Mobile retains Ochaka's centered brand, left menu, right actions, copy on the left, and model on the right, with a smooth overlay for readable text.

Reviews are labelled samples by the user's request. Service copy reflects the current pre-launch terms rather than unconfirmed round-the-clock support, refund deadlines or shipping destinations. Footer signup uses the existing newsletter endpoint and consent handling.

Verification: production build, TypeScript, ESLint, unit suite, both themes and languages, browser navigation/search/filter/saved-piece/quick-view interactions, mobile drawer and responsive section widths. Browser previews are under `previews/`.

Latest audit: mobile Lighthouse 81 performance, 100 accessibility, 100 best practices, and 100 SEO. Simulated mobile LCP was 4.4s, total blocking time 20ms, layout shift 0. This is a local production audit, not field telemetry. The headline performance target remains a further optimization opportunity.

Final responsive checks: no horizontal overflow on home at 320, 375, 768, 1024, 1440, 1920 and 2560 pixels; shop, product, About, Contact, cart launch state and privacy pages checked at 375 and 1440 pixels. New Sinhala content checked at 320 pixels. TypeScript, ESLint and 186 unit tests passed.

Autoplay advanced while the hero remained hovered, both white side circles became visible, scroll-down hid the header and scroll-up returned only the navigation. Final exports measure exactly 3840×2160. Tall-screen image `sizes` requests account for the actual landscape photograph crop rather than just the phone width. Final build, TypeScript and ESLint passed; the 186-test suite passed during this revision. Brand refinement: removed the boxed emblem from the navigation, leaving a larger responsive BRAKMASRA wordmark. Simplified the existing SVG favicon to a white B in a dark circle; Apple touch and installed-app PNG icons now use the same vector source through `scripts/prepare-brand-icons.mjs`. Header checks from 320 to 2560px show no overflow or wordmark/action overlap. Both theme colors, the favicon metadata link, TypeScript, ESLint and production build were verified. Preview: `previews/wordmark-desktop-light.jpg`.

Card image framing: collection, editorial and lookbook frames now use the source photos' 3:4 portrait ratio on desktop and mobile. All product, collection, lookbook, editorial, quick-view, gallery, thumbnail, cart and Shop-menu images use `object-fit: contain` so the entire image remains visible. Removed image zoom on collection, lookbook and Shop-menu hover. Mobile quick view retains a portrait frame rather than its former landscape crop. Shop checks at 320, 375, 768, 1024, 1440 and 2560px passed with every image contained and no horizontal overflow. Home collections and product gallery were checked at 390px. Production build passed. Screenshots: `previews/cards-collection-fit.jpg`, `cards-lookbook-fit.jpg`, `cards-quick-view-mobile-fit.jpg`.

Currency and language popups: replaced the fixed LKR label and native language select with shared Ochaka-style pointed popups. LKR/USD use locally hosted Sri Lankan/US SVG flags from the MIT-licensed flag-icons project; English/Sinhala use a plain text list. The 40px top bar keeps a single line with separators, and selected options use the brand red. Header, mobile drawer and footer share the saved currency; cookie persistence renders the selection on reload and local storage synchronizes tabs. Footer popups open upward. Arrow keys, Home/End, Escape and outside clicks are supported. Visibility changes immediately so keyboard focus is not delayed by the opening opacity animation.

Public browsing prices, quick view and cart totals can display estimated USD values from the cached ExchangeRate-API LKR feed when commerce is enabled. Amounts stay in integer minor units, estimates are labelled, and checkout keeps its canonical LKR charge. Missing or stale rates retain the actual currency label. Launch-gated prices remain unpublished.
